## Insight
Revenue is concentrated in AI, ML & DevOps, while the strongest profit contribution also comes from AI, ML & DevOps. This pattern suggests that the services with the highest commercial demand are also points where delivery discipline and pricing decisions matter most.
The delivery story is not uniform. Remote Infrastructure carries the greatest average delivery delay, suggesting that the service has more risk related to planning complexity, dependency management, or staffing mix.
The budget variance picture is also a management signal. With 36.0% of projects exceeding budget, the business should keep a closer watch on scope changes, cost assumptions, and project review checkpoints.
Customer experience is mostly healthy, but satisfaction falls below 70 for 1 record. These project outcomes should be reviewed in the context of project quality, service complexity, and delivery confidence.

## Recommendation
Management should create a focused capacity and estimation review for Remote Infrastructure before approving future high-complexity work.
Budget governance should include additional stage-gate checks for work that is close to or above budget, especially when scope changes are expected.
The customer success team should follow up with clients whose projects show low satisfaction and pair those reviews with delivery-or-team-quality checks.
Team utilization and productivity should be used as part of a weekly staffing review so project delivery remains balanced across services.
